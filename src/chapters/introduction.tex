\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Preamble}
The following living document summarizes the steps necessary to reproduce finite element simulation data from the DFWind2 project starting from the raw data provided by the manufacturer. The collaborative, cross-institutional documentation serves internal quality assurance purposes. All processes and necessary adjustment steps together with their justification are thus recorded transparently in a coherent document. As far as possible, all work steps are documented exclusively with software services that are freely accessible to all DLR employees. This ensures that the results presented can be reproduced at any time by anyone, anywhere. However, the raw data of the finite element model used are subject to confidentiality and are not part of this documentation. 
The document is divided into three chapters. The first chapter describes the necessary steps to create an executable model in ANSYS Workbench from the provided data. Any mesh adaptations of the model are also described here. The second chapter deals with the structural mechanical and/or aerodynamic analyses performed. In the last chapter, the results obtained are discussed and recorded for future DLR staff.
